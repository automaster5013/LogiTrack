# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

LogiTrack is committed to a welcoming, professional, and harassment-free community. Participation should be safe and productive regardless of experience, identity, background, or viewpoint.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and constructive in technical discussion.
- Critique ideas and implementations, not people.
- Welcome questions and provide context instead of assuming prior knowledge.
- Respect privacy and do not publish credentials, personal data, or private communications.
- Accept maintainer decisions about scope, safety, and community boundaries gracefully.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, insults, deliberate disruption, doxxing, and repeated unwanted contact are not tolerated. Publishing another person's private information or encouraging unsafe access to systems is also prohibited.

## Enforcement

For ordinary project concerns, contact the maintainer privately through the repository owner's GitHub profile. Security vulnerabilities must use the private process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, comments, issues, or other participation that violates this policy. Depending on severity and history, enforcement may include a warning, temporary restriction, or permanent ban from project spaces. Reports will be reviewed as promptly and confidentially as practical, with conflicts of interest disclosed and avoided.

This policy applies in the repository, its discussions, issue tracker, review threads, and project-related public spaces.
