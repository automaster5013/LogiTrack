# Security Policy

## Supported version

LogiTrack is maintained from the `main` branch. Security fixes are applied to the current `main` revision; older commits and locally modified deployments are not supported separately.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please do not disclose suspected vulnerabilities in a public issue, discussion, or pull request.

Use GitHub's [Report a vulnerability](https://github.com/automaster5013/LogiTrack/security/advisories/new) form so the report and follow-up remain private. Include:

- the affected component and revision;
- steps or a minimal proof of concept that reproduces the issue;
- the expected impact and any known prerequisites;
- suggested mitigations, if available.

The maintainer will acknowledge a complete report within 72 hours and will use the private advisory to coordinate validation, remediation, and disclosure. The time required for a fix depends on severity and complexity; no public disclosure date should be assumed until it is agreed in the advisory.

## Research guidelines

Use only accounts, data, and infrastructure you own or have explicit permission to test. Do not access other users' data, degrade service availability, use social engineering, or retain secrets or personal data encountered during research. Stop testing and report immediately if sensitive data is exposed.

Good-faith research that follows this policy is welcomed. This policy does not authorize activity that violates applicable law or third-party terms.
